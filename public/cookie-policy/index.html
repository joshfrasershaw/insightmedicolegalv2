What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small files that websites can save in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, tracking pixels and scripts that read information on your device. UK privacy rules apply to many of these technologies, not just cookies.

The Glasgow-inspired hero artwork is loaded locally. An architectural photograph elsewhere on the page is requested from a Webflow-hosted Amazon S3 address. The provider may receive normal request information such as your IP address and browser details. These requests are not the same as opting in to advertising cookies. See our privacy notice for more information about data sharing.

Managing cookies

This site does not currently deploy optional tracking. The cookie notice can be reopened using the Cookie notice control in the footer. If optional tracking is introduced, the site must seek consent before enabling it.

The cookie notice is informational, not a request for consent to non-essential cookies. You can clear the saved notice dismissal through your browser settings to see it again on your next visit.

You can also clear this site's browser storage or block cookies in your browser settings. Some essential hosting security features may then behave differently.

If the site changes

If analytics, advertising or other optional services are added, the site must explain what each tool does, identify the provider and ask for a new choice before loading it. A choice made today is not permission for undisclosed future tracking.
