PSYCHIATRIC EXPERT REPORTS

Professional regulation

Psychiatric reports for professional regulators and fitness-to-practise cases.

What a report can cover

  • Whether there is a psychiatric condition.
  • Any treatment and how the person is managing.
  • The possible effect of the condition on their work.
  • Any relevant mental health risks.
The regulator, not the psychiatrist, makes the final decision.
