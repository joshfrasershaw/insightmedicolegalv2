ABOUT THE PRACTICE

Dr Yameen Qureshi

Consultant psychiatrist specialising in general adult and addiction psychiatry.

Professional background

Dr Qureshi was a consultant psychiatrist with NHS Lanarkshire from 2010 to 2022. He is now lead clinician for general adult and addiction psychiatry at Priory Hospital Glasgow.

He prepares independent psychiatric reports for legal cases, insurers and professional regulators.

His role in expert witness work is to provide an independent, clearly reasoned psychiatric opinion based on the evidence available.
